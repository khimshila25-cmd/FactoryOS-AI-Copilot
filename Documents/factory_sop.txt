Factory Production SOP

1. Fabric Inspection
All incoming fabric must be inspected before production.

2. Cutting Process
Cutting should begin only after quality approval.

3. Stitching Process
Each batch must be tracked by production ID.

4. Delay Management
Orders delayed more than 3 days must be escalated to the production manager.

5. Supplier Risk
Suppliers with more than 2 late deliveries in a month should be flagged as high risk.
